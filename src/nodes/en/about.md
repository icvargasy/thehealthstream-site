The Healthstream is a free, open-access registry that decodes how your body works — mapping feedback loops across biology, lifestyle, and longevity science, evaluated by evidence strength.

### The Systems Biology Lens
We view the human body not as a collection of isolated symptoms, but as an interconnected circuit board of feedback loops. Every decoding maps these inputs, transducers, and outcomes upstream and downstream so readers can make deliberate, evidence-informed decisions.

### 3-Tier Epistemic Transparency

Health and longevity research moves continuously from raw hypothesis to clinical consensus. We evaluate all scientific research across three constructive confidence tiers:

- <span class="detail-grade-badge tier-consensus-core">HIGH / MODERATE — PROVEN</span> Strong, proven science backed by clinical studies in humans.
- <span class="detail-grade-badge tier-emerging-frontier">LOW — PROMISING</span> Promising science shown in early tests or animal studies, awaiting larger trials.
- <span class="detail-grade-badge tier-exploratory-sandbox">VERY LOW — UNTESTED</span> Experimental or unproven ideas based on biofeedback or theoretical concepts.

### Community-Driven Co-Creation

Science is a collaborative endeavor. Our library is co-curated with our reader community through open interaction loops:

- **[Pipeline Upvoting](./backlog.html)** &mdash; Visitors directly vote on proposed topics to prioritize research and static compilation.
- **[Pathway Submissions](./submit-proposal.html)** &mdash; Anyone can submit new questions or protocol ideas for citation auditing.
- **[Lexicon Verification](./vocabulary.html)** &mdash; Readers and human domain experts verify AI-compiled jargon definitions.

### Editorial Independence
Our content curation and evidence ratings are 100% independent. Core pathway decodings, jargon definitions, and evidence reviews remain completely open-access and free of paywalls. Editorial decisions are strictly decoupled from commercial influences or affiliate partnerships.
